**_«If we opened people up, we would find landscapes»_**

Starting from the sentence by filmmaker _Agnès Varda_, the project explores the archive as a device of transformation. 

The work began with the glass plates of local photographer Miquel Martí Florensa, preserved in Tàrrega, Catalonia: portraits and scenes of everyday life, originally fixed with clarity and intention. 
\
Searching further into the archive, I found many plates that deterioration, scratches, stains had rendered unrecognizable. It is here that the archive stops being a place of conservation alone and revealed itself as something still active, a device of transformation working on the image from within.

I photographed these unrecognizable plates, and in the resulting images the human figure dissolves into what I read as abstract landscapes, giving literal form to Varda's words: the personal opens onto something universal, a space of transit between memory and loss, revealing the fragility of both matter and the communities it contains.

_Si obríssim les persones, hi trobaríem paisatges_ was developed during a residency for [TRANSDOCUMENTAR 2026](https://xac-gencat-cat.translate.goog/ca/llista_arxius_comarcals/urgell/detall/Nou-article-00252?_x_tr_sl=ca&_x_tr_tl=en&_x_tr_hl=en&_x_tr_pto=sc), in collaboration with the [Arxiu Comarcal de l'Urgell](https://xac.gencat.cat/ca/llista_arxius_comarcals/urgell/).
